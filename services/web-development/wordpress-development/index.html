FAQ

Is WordPress suitable for a small business?

Yes. It is one of the most widely used website platforms — flexible and with many ready-made features.

Do I need to update anything regularly?

WordPress and its plugins are updated regularly. We can take this on as part of monthly maintenance.

Can I change the design later?

In most cases yes — the theme can be replaced or reworked without losing your content.
