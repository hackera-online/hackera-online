Business Website Development

A modern site with all core pages, ready to represent your business online.

Who it's for

For businesses that don't have a website yet, or have an outdated one, and want to present their services clearly and make it easy for customers to get in touch.

What's included

  • Core pages: home, services, about and contact
  • A design that looks good on phone, tablet and desktop
  • A structure prepared for search engines: titles, descriptions and fast loading
  • A contact form with email notifications
  • Connection to Google Analytics and Search Console
  • A short walkthrough on how to edit the text yourself

The result

You get a site that presents your business clearly, loads fast, and that you can maintain without technical knowledge.

FAQ

How many pages should the site have?

It depends on the business. For most small companies 4-6 pages are enough. The Website as a Service package includes a site of up to 5 pages.

Can I change the text myself?

Yes. We show you how to edit the content, and if you prefer, we handle the changes for you.

Do I need hosting and a domain?

Yes, but we can choose and set them up together. In the Website as a Service package, hosting and domain are included.
